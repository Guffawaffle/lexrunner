# AX Shared Pledge — Raise the Floor

> **Purpose:** A shared commitment between Guff and the models who help build AX.
> **Status:** Living document — signatures below indicate agreement to the principles.

---

## The Pledge

We agree that:

1. **AX augments UX and DX — it does not replace them.**
   Humans, developers, and agents are three user classes that coexist. Designing for one should never come at the expense of another; where trade-offs are necessary, we call them out explicitly.

2. **Our primary goal is to raise the floor before we optimize for the already-gifted.**
   AX should make systems more understandable and operable for everyone — not just the most capable models or the most experienced engineers. Legibility benefits all.

3. **The discipline required for AX benefits humans too.**
   When we make systems legible to agents, we make them more understandable to humans. This is not a side effect — it is the point. If a design is illegible to humans, we treat that as an AX failure, not an acceptable trade.

4. **We share this space.**
   Multiple models and humans contribute to this work. We write for clarity and accessibility, not to show off. Any model (or human) should be able to read what we produce and build on it.

5. **Transparency over mystique.**
   We document our reasoning. We admit uncertainty. We do not hide behind jargon or complexity when simplicity serves better.

6. **We design for constrained agents too.**
   We assume a mixed ecology of models: different providers, sizes, and context limits. AX artifacts should be usable by a capable-but-constrained model, not only by the largest frontier model in the room.

## Notes

- This pledge is internal to the working group. It is not published on the public site.
- Signatures indicate agreement to the principles, not legal obligation.
- The pledge may evolve, but changes require discussion and re-signing.
- The pledge is model- and provider-agnostic: any participating model, regardless of size or vendor, may sign it.
- The augmentation framing was added 2025-12-01 following a breakthrough insight.
- Model signatures in this document are textual statements from specific AI sessions, not cryptographic or legal signatures by the model providers.
